2. COSINE — HORIZONTAL SURVIVAL
####################################################################

QUESTION ASKED:

"Does cosine define horizontal length?"

ANSWER:
YES.

FORMULA:

cos(θ) = adjacent / hypotenuse

REARRANGED:

adjacent = cos(θ) × hypotenuse

MEANING:

Cosine extracts horizontal component.

It answers:

"How much horizontal survives from slanted direction?"


--------------------------------------------------------------------
DEEP INTERPRETATION DEVELOPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------

You developed:

"same-direction survival"

Meaning:
- original direction survival
- horizontal preservation
- horizontal projection

This was a major intuition breakthrough.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT REALIZATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------

At 0°:

fully horizontal

cos(0°)=1

ALL survives horizontally.


At 90°:

fully vertical

cos(90°)=0

NO horizontal survives.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------

hypotenuse = 10
angle = 60°

cos(60°)=0.5

horizontal = 10 × 0.5 = 5

Only 5 units survive horizontally.

6. TANGENT — SLOPE / STEEPNESS
####################################################################

QUESTION ASKED:

"If sine is vertical and cosine horizontal,
then what does tangent define?"

FORMULA:

tan(θ)= sin(θ)/cos(θ)

MEANING:

tan(θ)= vertical / horizontal

THIS is slope.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
SLOPE FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------------------------

m = Δy / Δx

Meaning:
vertical change per horizontal change.


--------------------------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLES
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Flat line:

m=0/5=0

45° line:

m=1/1=1

Steep line:

m=1/0.01=100


--------------------------------------------------------------------
IMPORTANT REALIZATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------

Tangent is NOT a coordinate.

It measures:
- steepness
- directional aggression
- rise/run relationship
