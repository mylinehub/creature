📘 MASTER VISUAL DOCUMENT: Surfaces & Contours

🧠 1. CORE IDEA (READ THIS FIRST)
We always start with:
[z = f(x, y)]
This is a surface (3D shape)
A contour is made by fixing height:
[f(x,y) = c]
👉 So:
3D surface → slice at height → 2D contour

🔴 6. SADDLE (Twisted Surface)
￼
￼
￼
￼
￼
Equation
[z = x^2 - y^2]
What it means
along x → goes UP
along y → goes DOWN
👉 mixed behavior

Contours
[x^2 - y^2 = c]

Cases
(c > 0)
👉 hyperbola (left-right)
(c < 0)
👉 hyperbola (up-down)
(c = 0)
👉 lines (y = x, y = -x)

Why hyperbola?
Because:
one term adds
one term subtracts
👉 they compete

Real feeling
👉 mountain pass (up one way, down another)
